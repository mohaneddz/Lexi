# Cover art sources

## Photographed covers (public domain)

- `dict-webster-essentials.jpg` — binding of *Webster's International Dictionary*,
  scanned from *Webster's Unabridged Dictionary, Volume I*.
  Source: Wikimedia Commons, `File:Webster's Unabridged Dictionary Volume I.pdf` (page 1).
  Public domain.

- `trans-countries-en-es.jpg`, `trans-countries-en-fr.jpg` — *A New and Accurate Map of
  the Known World*, Emanuel Bowen, 1744.
  Source: Wikimedia Commons, `File:1744 Bowen Map of the World in Hemispheres -
  Geographicus - World-bowen-1744.jpg`. Public domain.

## Designed covers

- `dict-wordnet-*.svg` — drawn for this app.

  Princeton WordNet is a lexical database, not a printed book, so there is no real
  cover to photograph. These are typographic covers over a word-graph motif rather
  than an invented book jacket.
