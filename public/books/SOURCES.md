# Book sources

Every book in `packs/` comes from an openly licensed dictionary. The field,
general and monolingual books are regenerated by `scripts/books/build_books.py`
from the downloads made by `scripts/books/fetch_sources.py`.

| Books | Source | License |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `wikt-*-en-*` field books, `wikt-general-*` | [Wiktionary](https://en.wiktionary.org/) (English edition), extracted by [Wiktextract](https://github.com/tatuylonen/wiktextract) and published at [kaikki.org](https://kaikki.org/dictionary/) | [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) and GFDL |
| `wikt-mono-fr`, `wikt-mono-de`, `wikt-mono-es`, `wikt-mono-zh` | The French, German, Spanish and Chinese Wiktionaries, via Wiktextract on kaikki.org | CC BY-SA 4.0 and GFDL |
| `cedict-zh-en` | [CC-CEDICT](https://www.mdbg.net/chinese/dictionary?page=cedict), maintained by MDBG | [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) |
| `dict-webster-essentials` | Webster's Unabridged Dictionary (1913) | Public domain |
| `dict-wordnet-*`, `dict-topic-*` | [Princeton WordNet](https://wordnet.princeton.edu/), via nltk_data | [WordNet License](https://wordnet.princeton.edu/license-and-commercial-use) |
| `trans-countries-*` | [mledoze/countries](https://github.com/mledoze/countries) | ODbL |
| `swadesh-*` | Wiktionary's Swadesh list appendices | CC BY-SA 4.0 |

## How the Wiktionary books are cut

- **Field books** take every English word whose sense Wiktionary tags with
  that field (for example `computing` or the `Computing` category) and has a
  translation into the target language, plus every native word tagged with
  the field and glossed in English. Parent tags that Wiktionary adds
  automatically (a computing term is also tagged `mathematics`) are ignored
  when a more specific field applies.
- **General dictionaries** rank English senses by how many languages
  translate them, as a stand-in for how common they are, and keep the top
  25,000.
- **Direct pairs into Arabic** pair a German, French, Spanish or Chinese word
  with Arabic when both translate the same English sense.
- **Monolingual dictionaries** keep up to 25,000 headwords with native
  definitions, everyday words first (those that appear in translation tables),
  and drop entries that only point at another form ("plural of ...").
